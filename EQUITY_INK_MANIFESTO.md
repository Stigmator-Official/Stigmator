### THE TECHNOLOGY

**Partnership Codes:**
- Format: `INK-[ARTIST]-[YEAR]-[CODE]`
- Example: `INK-ALEX-2024-X7K9`
- Single-use or multi-use
- Expirable or perpetual
- Revocable by artist

**Smart Contracts (Simplified):**
```sql
ON garment_sale:
  IF design_has_partnership:
    SPLIT revenue:
      - Artist: X%
      - Partner(s): Y%  
      - Platform: Z%
    NOTIFY all parties
    UPDATE earnings dashboards
```

**Verification:**
- Photo of tattoo required
- Artist approval
- Optional: Geolocation of tattoo shop
- Optional: Timestamp of session
